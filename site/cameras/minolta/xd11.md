The Minolta XD11 (known as XD7 in some markets) was a groundbreaking 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1977, featuring the world's first dual-mode automatic exposure system. This innovative camera offered both aperture-priority and shutter-priority auto exposure modes in a single body, along with full manual control and an accurate center-weighted metering system. The XD11 incorporated advanced electronic control with mechanical backup, ensuring reliability even with battery failure. Its sophisticated exposure system, combined with excellent build quality and compatibility with Minolta's SR lens mount, made it highly regarded among serious photographers. The camera's dual-mode automation was revolutionary for its time, appealing to professional photographers and advanced enthusiasts who appreciated having multiple automatic exposure options while retaining full manual control when creative situations demanded it.
