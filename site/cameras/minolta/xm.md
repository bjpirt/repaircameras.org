The Minolta XM was a professional 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1972, representing Minolta's premium manual-focus offering designed to compete with top-tier professional cameras. This robust camera featured interchangeable viewfinders, including both prism and waist-level options, along with comprehensive professional accessories and system compatibility. The XM incorporated precise through-the-lens metering with multiple metering patterns and offered full manual control over all exposure settings. Built with exceptional durability and mechanical precision, it used Minolta's SR lens mount and featured a reliable focal plane shutter system. Targeted at professional photographers and serious enthusiasts, the XM was particularly valued for its modular design, allowing customization for specific photographic applications. Its professional build quality, extensive accessory system, and reliable performance made it a favorite among professionals who demanded uncompromising quality and system flexibility.
