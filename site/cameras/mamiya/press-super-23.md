The Mamiya Press Super 23, produced from 1964 to the early 1980s, was a professional press camera designed for journalists and commercial photographers who needed reliable, high-quality medium format results under demanding conditions. This rangefinder camera shoots 6x9cm images on 120 film, providing exceptional image quality and eight exposures per roll. The camera features interchangeable lenses, roll film and sheet film capability, and a coupled rangefinder system for precise focusing. Built like a tank with robust mechanical construction, the Super 23 could withstand the rigors of professional use while delivering consistently sharp images. Its leaf shutter system enabled flash synchronization at all speeds, making it ideal for press photography and studio work where reliability was paramount.
