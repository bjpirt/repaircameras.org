The Mamiya RZ67, launched in 1982, advanced the professional medium format system concept with electronic automation while maintaining the modular flexibility that made the RB67 legendary. This 6x7cm format SLR introduced electronic shutter control, automatic exposure modes, and improved ergonomics while retaining full compatibility with most RB67 accessories. The camera features interchangeable film backs, lenses, viewfinders, and focusing screens, plus an electronic bellows focusing system for precise macro work. Advanced metering options include spot, center-weighted, and multi-zone patterns, making it adaptable to various shooting conditions. Primarily designed for studio work, the RZ67 became the preferred choice for fashion photographers, portraitists, and commercial studios who demanded both technical precision and operational efficiency in their medium format workflow.
