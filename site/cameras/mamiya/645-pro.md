The Mamiya 645 Pro, introduced in the early 1990s, represents the pinnacle of Mamiya's 645 format system. This professional medium format SLR camera produces 15 exposures on 120 film, offering an excellent balance between image quality and portability. The modular system features interchangeable film backs, viewfinders, and focusing screens, making it incredibly versatile for studio and location work. With its reliable leaf shutter lenses and precise metering system, the 645 Pro became a favorite among wedding photographers, portraitists, and commercial photographers who needed medium format quality with 35mm-like handling. The camera's robust construction and extensive accessory system established it as a professional standard.
