The Mamiya C220, produced from 1968 to 2001, is a professional twin-lens reflex camera that revolutionized the TLR format with its interchangeable lens system. Unlike traditional fixed-lens TLRs, the C220 features removable matched pairs of taking and viewing lenses, offering focal lengths from 55mm to 250mm. This 6x6cm format camera incorporates bellows focusing for close-up work and precise composition control. The parallax-corrected viewing system and bright focusing screen made it popular among portrait and studio photographers. Built with exceptional mechanical precision and reliability, the C220 became the professional standard for TLR photography, bridging the gap between the simplicity of twin-lens design and the versatility demanded by professional applications.
