The Mamiya RB67, introduced in 1970, revolutionized professional medium format photography with its innovative modular design and 6x7cm format that maximized film usage. This studio-oriented SLR camera system features interchangeable film backs, lenses, viewfinders, and focusing screens, allowing photographers to adapt quickly to different shooting requirements. The camera's bellows focusing system provides macro capability and precise composition control, while the leaf shutter lenses offer flash synchronization at all speeds. Weighing substantially more than 35mm cameras, the RB67 was designed primarily for tripod use in studio and controlled environments. Its exceptional image quality, mechanical reliability, and comprehensive accessory system made it the backbone of professional portrait, fashion, and commercial photography studios worldwide for decades.
