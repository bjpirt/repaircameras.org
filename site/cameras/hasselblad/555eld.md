The Hasselblad 555ELD represented the company's venture into electronic medium format SLR cameras with advanced automation features during the 1980s. This professional 6x6cm camera incorporated sophisticated electronic exposure control systems with multiple metering patterns and automatic exposure modes, while maintaining Hasselblad's legendary build quality and modular design philosophy. The 555ELD featured enhanced electronic circuits for precise shutter timing and advanced light metering capabilities that appealed to professionals seeking automated precision. Like all Hasselblad cameras, it accepted the complete range of Carl Zeiss lenses and system accessories, providing incredible versatility for various photography applications. The camera became popular among professionals who required the reliability and image quality of Hasselblad engineering combined with modern electronic conveniences, particularly in studio environments where consistent, repeatable results were essential for commercial and artistic photography applications.
