The Hasselblad 205FCC, introduced in the early 1990s, represented the most advanced focal plane shutter camera in Hasselblad's lineup, featuring comprehensive electronic automation and color correction capabilities. This sophisticated 6x6cm medium format SLR incorporated advanced metering systems with multiple exposure modes and precise electronic shutter control. The "FCC" designation indicated its advanced color correction metering system, designed to provide exceptional accuracy for both film and early digital applications. Built with Hasselblad's signature modular architecture, the 205FCC accepted the complete range of Carl Zeiss lenses and professional accessories. The camera featured enhanced weather sealing, improved ergonomics, and advanced electronics that made it particularly popular among studio photographers and professionals requiring the ultimate in exposure precision and system flexibility for demanding commercial and artistic applications.
