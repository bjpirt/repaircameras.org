The Hasselblad 201F, introduced in the early 1980s, represented a significant advancement in the company's medium format SLR system with its electronic focal plane shutter. This professional 6x6cm camera featured precise electronic shutter control with speeds from 16 seconds to 1/2000th second, offering exceptional accuracy for demanding photography applications. The 201F maintained Hasselblad's signature modular design philosophy, accepting the full range of Carl Zeiss lenses, interchangeable viewfinders, and film magazines. Popular among studio photographers and professionals requiring precise exposure control, the camera incorporated modern electronics while preserving the mechanical reliability that made Hasselblad famous. Its robust construction and advanced shutter technology made it a preferred choice for technical photography where precise timing was critical.
