The Hasselblad 202FA, launched in the mid-1980s, evolved from the 201F with enhanced features for professional photography applications. This sophisticated 6x6cm medium format SLR incorporated an advanced electronic focal plane shutter system with even greater precision and reliability than its predecessor. The camera featured improved electronics, better weather sealing, and enhanced durability for demanding field use. Like all Hasselblad cameras, the 202FA utilized the company's renowned modular system, accepting Carl Zeiss lenses and various accessories. Professional photographers valued its consistent performance in studio and location work, while its electronic automation helped streamline complex shooting scenarios. The 202FA represented Hasselblad's commitment to evolving their traditional mechanical excellence with modern electronic precision, maintaining their position at the forefront of professional medium format photography.
