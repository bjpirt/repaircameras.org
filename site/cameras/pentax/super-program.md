The Pentax Super Program is an advanced 35mm SLR camera from 1982, featuring innovative programmed auto exposure with manual override capability. This camera introduced Pentax's unique "shift program" system, allowing photographers to change the aperture/shutter speed combination while maintaining correct exposure in program mode. Features include center-weighted and spot metering, aperture-priority, and full manual modes, plus compatibility with Pentax's extensive K-mount lens system. The Super Program's bright viewfinder displays comprehensive exposure information, while its solid construction ensures reliable operation. Notable for its ergonomic design and intuitive controls, this camera bridged the gap between mechanical precision and electronic convenience. The innovative program shift feature provided creative flexibility rarely found in automatic cameras of the era. Its combination of advanced automation with complete manual control made it popular among both professionals and enthusiasts seeking versatile, reliable performance.
