The Pentax K2 is a professional 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1975, representing the pinnacle of Pentax's mechanical camera engineering. Featuring full manual control with match-needle exposure metering, the K2 offers shutter speeds from 1/1000s to 8 seconds plus bulb mode. Its robust, all-metal construction includes a bright viewfinder with interchangeable focusing screens and comprehensive exposure information display. The camera introduced several innovations including multiple exposure capability and improved mirror dampening for reduced vibration. Compatible with the extensive K-mount lens system, the K2 became popular among professional photographers for its reliability and precision. Its mechanical operation independent of batteries (except for metering) made it ideal for demanding conditions. The K2's exceptional build quality and professional features established it as one of the finest manual focus SLRs ever produced.
