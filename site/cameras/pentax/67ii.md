The Pentax 67II is an advanced medium format SLR camera producing 6×7cm images on 120/220 film. Released in 1998 as an evolution of the original 6×7 system, it features built-in TTL flash metering, improved ergonomics, and enhanced reliability. The camera maintains the distinctive mirror-up operation and leaf shutter compatibility of its predecessors while adding modern conveniences like multiple exposure capability and improved viewfinder optics. With its extensive selection of high-quality SMC Pentax lenses ranging from ultra-wide to super-telephoto, the 67II became favored by landscape photographers and professionals requiring maximum image quality. Its robust construction and exceptional optical performance made it one of the last great manual focus medium format systems before digital transition.
