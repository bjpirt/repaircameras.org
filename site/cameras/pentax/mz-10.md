The Pentax MZ-10 is an advanced autofocus 35mm SLR camera from 1996, designed for serious enthusiasts and semi-professional photographers. Features include sophisticated multi-pattern metering, predictive autofocus tracking, and comprehensive exposure modes from full program to manual control. The camera offers unique capabilities like hyper-manual mode that combines manual focusing with auto exposure, and hyper-program mode allowing instant manual override. Its robust construction includes weather sealing and a bright pentaprism viewfinder with extensive exposure information. Compatible with all K-mount lenses, the MZ-10 provides excellent handling ergonomics and intuitive controls. The camera's advanced features, including spot metering and multiple exposure modes, made it popular among photographers transitioning from manual to autofocus systems while maintaining creative control and professional build quality.
