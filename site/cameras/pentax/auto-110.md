The Pentax Auto 110 is a revolutionary miniature SLR camera system that uses 110 cartridge film, introduced in 1978. Despite its tiny size, it features full SLR functionality including through-the-lens viewing, interchangeable lenses, and a complete system of accessories. The camera offers programmed auto exposure with manual override capability and includes an electronic self-timer. With its comprehensive lens lineup ranging from 18mm wide-angle to 70mm telephoto, the Auto 110 made SLR photography accessible in an ultra-compact format. Popular among travelers and street photographers who needed discretion without sacrificing creative control, this innovative camera proved that serious photography could come in a pocket-sized package. Its unique position as the world's smallest SLR system makes it a fascinating collectible today.
