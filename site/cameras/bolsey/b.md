The Bolsey Model B is a compact 35mm rangefinder camera produced in the 1940s and early 1950s by the Bolsey Corporation of America. This distinctive camera features a unique dual-spring motor drive system that advances the film and cocks the shutter simultaneously. The Model B offers shutter speeds from 1/25s to 1/200s plus Time and Bulb modes, with a coupled rangefinder for precise focusing. Its Wollensak Anastigmat f/3.2 lens delivers sharp images, while the camera's Art Deco styling and aluminum construction make it both durable and aesthetically appealing. Popular among amateur photographers of its era.
