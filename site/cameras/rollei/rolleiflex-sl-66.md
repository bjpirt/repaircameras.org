The Rolleiflex SL 66 is a professional medium format single-lens reflex camera introduced in 1966, featuring a distinctive modular design and 6x6cm square format. This innovative camera incorporates a tilting lens standard for perspective control, interchangeable film backs, and a comprehensive system of viewfinders and focusing screens. The SL 66 uses high-quality Carl Zeiss and Schneider lenses in Rollei's proprietary mount, with focal plane shutter speeds from 1 second to 1/1000 second. Its professional features include mirror lock-up, depth-of-field preview, and precise ground glass focusing. Designed for studio and architectural photography, the SL 66's tilt mechanism and modular system made it particularly valuable for commercial photographers requiring perspective correction and flexibility, establishing it as a unique alternative to traditional view cameras.
