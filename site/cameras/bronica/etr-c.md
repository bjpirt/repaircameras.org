The Bronica ETR-C is a compact medium format SLR camera that produces 6x4.5cm images on 120/220 film, introduced in the late 1970s. This electronically controlled camera features aperture-priority automatic exposure with manual override, making it user-friendly while maintaining professional capabilities. The focal plane shutter provides speeds from 8s to 1/500s plus Bulb mode, with flash sync at 1/60s. The camera system includes interchangeable lenses, viewfinders, and film backs, along with a built-in motor drive option. Its compact size and lighter weight compared to 6x6 systems made it popular among wedding and portrait photographers seeking professional quality in a more portable package.
