The Bronica ETRSi is an improved version of the ETRS medium format SLR system, introduced in 1989 with enhanced electronic features and ergonomics. This 6x4.5cm camera incorporates a more sophisticated exposure system with spot metering capability and improved viewfinder displays. The focal plane shutter operates from 8s to 1/500s plus Bulb mode, with flash sync at 1/60s. Notable improvements include a larger, brighter viewfinder, better weather sealing, and compatibility with electronic flash systems. The ETRSi maintains full compatibility with ETRS lenses and accessories while offering refined handling and more precise metering, making it a popular choice for professional studio and location work.
