The Olympus OM-2 is a sophisticated 35mm SLR camera that revolutionized automatic exposure control. It features aperture-priority automation with manual override capability, using an innovative off-the-film (OTF) metering system that measures light directly from the film plane during exposure. This advanced metering provides exceptional accuracy in challenging lighting conditions. The camera offers shutter speeds from 1/1000s to 60s, plus manual speeds and bulb mode. Its compact, lightweight design and quiet operation made it popular among street photographers and photojournalists. The OM-2 accepts Zuiko lenses via the OM mount system, known for exceptional optical quality and comprehensive focal length range.
