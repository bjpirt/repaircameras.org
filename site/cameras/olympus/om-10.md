The Olympus OM-10 was an entry-level 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1979, designed to make the renowned OM system accessible to amateur photographers. This compact, lightweight camera featured aperture-priority automatic exposure with an optional manual adapter, allowing users to grow their skills progressively. The OM-10's innovative OTF (Off-The-Film) flash metering system provided accurate flash exposures, while its bright viewfinder and match-needle display made composition and exposure settings clear and intuitive. Compatible with the full range of Zuiko lenses, the OM-10 offered the optical excellence of the professional OM system at an affordable price point. Its reliable electronic shutter and solid build quality made it a popular choice for photography students and enthusiasts throughout the 1980s.
