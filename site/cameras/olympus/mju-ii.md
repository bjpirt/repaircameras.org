The Olympus Mju-II (known as the Stylus Epic in North America) was a premium compact 35mm camera launched in 1997, renowned for its exceptional image quality and weatherproof construction. Featuring a sharp 35mm f/2.8 Zuiko lens and advanced autofocus system, it delivered professional-grade results in an ultra-compact, clamshell design. The camera's weather-sealed body could withstand rain, snow, and harsh conditions, making it ideal for outdoor photography and travel. With its intelligent multi-zone metering, red-eye reduction flash, and self-timer, the Mju-II became a favorite among both amateur and professional photographers seeking a reliable backup camera. Its legendary build quality and optical performance established it as one of the finest compact cameras ever produced.
