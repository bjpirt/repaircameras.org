The Olympus OM-20 (known as the OM-G in some markets) was an entry-level 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1984, representing Olympus's commitment to making quality photography accessible. This fully automatic camera featured program mode exposure control, eliminating the complexity of manual settings for beginning photographers. Despite its simplified operation, the OM-20 maintained the compact dimensions and excellent build quality that characterized the OM system. The camera's electronic program mode automatically selected optimal aperture and shutter speed combinations, while its match-LED viewfinder display provided clear exposure information. Compatible with the extensive Zuiko lens system, the OM-20 offered newcomers an affordable entry point into professional-quality optics and the renowned OM system's exceptional handling and reliability.
