The Canon EOS 620 is a professional 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1987, representing Canon's second-generation EOS system with enhanced autofocus capabilities. This camera features an improved single-point autofocus system with greater accuracy and speed compared to the original EOS 650. The EOS 620 offers multiple exposure modes including program, aperture priority, shutter priority, and manual, with sophisticated TTL metering. Notable for its robust construction and professional-grade features, it includes multiple exposure capability and custom function settings. Targeted at professional and advanced amateur photographers, the EOS 620 established Canon's reputation for reliable autofocus performance in demanding shooting conditions while maintaining full compatibility with the growing EF lens system.
