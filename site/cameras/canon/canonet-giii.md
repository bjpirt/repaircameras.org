The Canon Canonet GIII is a sophisticated 35mm rangefinder camera released in 1972, representing the pinnacle of Canon's Canonet series. This camera features a bright coupled rangefinder with automatic exposure control via CdS light meter and offers both aperture priority and manual exposure modes. The GIII is equipped with a high-quality 40mm f/1.7 Canon lens and includes flash synchronization capabilities. Distinguished by its robust construction and precise metering system, it was targeted at serious amateur photographers who appreciated the compact size and quiet operation of rangefinders while demanding professional-level features and image quality.
