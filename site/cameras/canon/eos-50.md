The Canon EOS 50 (marketed as EOS ELAN II in North America) is an advanced amateur 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1995, featuring sophisticated autofocus and exposure control systems. This camera includes Canon's advanced 3-point autofocus system with eye-controlled focus point selection, allowing photographers to select focus points simply by looking at them through the viewfinder. The EOS 50 offers multiple exposure modes including program, aperture priority, shutter priority, and manual, plus specialized subject modes. Targeted at serious amateur photographers seeking professional features in a lighter body, it represented Canon's commitment to innovative user interfaces and advanced automation in the consumer SLR market.
