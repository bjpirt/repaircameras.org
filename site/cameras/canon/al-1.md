The Canon AL-1 is a compact 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1982, designed as an entry-level model featuring automated exposure control. This camera offers aperture priority and manual exposure modes with TTL center-weighted metering system. The AL-1 accepts Canon FD lenses and features a simplified control layout optimized for ease of use. Notable for its lightweight construction and affordable price point, it includes an improved version of Canon's Quick-Load film system. The AL-1 was specifically targeted at beginning photographers and budget-conscious consumers who wanted access to the Canon FD lens system without the complexity and cost of more advanced models.
