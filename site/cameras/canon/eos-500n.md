The Canon EOS 500N (marketed as EOS Rebel G in North America) is an entry-level 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1996, designed to make autofocus SLR photography accessible to beginning photographers. This camera features a simplified control layout with automatic and creative exposure modes, 3-point autofocus system, and built-in flash. The EOS 500N offers program, aperture priority, and full manual modes along with subject-specific scene modes for portraits, landscapes, and close-ups. Targeted at families and amateur photographers stepping up from point-and-shoot cameras, it provided an affordable entry into the Canon EOS system while maintaining compatibility with the full range of EF lenses.
