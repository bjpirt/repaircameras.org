The Canon EOS 650 is a landmark 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1987, representing Canon's revolutionary entry into autofocus SLR photography with the new EOS system. This groundbreaking camera features Canon's first autofocus system with electronic lens mount (EF mount), abandoning the mechanical FD mount for fully electronic communication between camera and lens. The EOS 650 offers program, aperture priority, shutter priority, and manual exposure modes with sophisticated TTL metering. Notable for introducing the electronic lens mount concept that enabled advanced features like in-lens autofocus motors, it was targeted at photographers ready to embrace the future of SLR technology, establishing the foundation for Canon's ongoing EOS system.
