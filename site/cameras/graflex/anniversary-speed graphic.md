The Graflex Anniversary Speed Graphic was a special commemorative edition of the iconic 4x5 press camera, celebrating decades of Speed Graphic excellence in photojournalism. Released in the 1950s, this model featured enhanced construction quality and refined mechanics compared to standard Speed Graphics. The camera maintained the classic folding design with front-mounted rangefinder, focal-plane shutter, and versatile lens standards that made the Speed Graphic the preferred choice of newspaper photographers worldwide. The Anniversary model represented the pinnacle of press camera development, incorporating years of field experience and user feedback. Its robust construction and reliable operation made it an indispensable tool for documenting history, from war zones to sporting events.
