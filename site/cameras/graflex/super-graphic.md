The Graflex Super Graphic represented the final evolution of the legendary Speed Graphic line, introduced in the 1950s as the ultimate press camera. This 4x5 format camera combined decades of refinement with modern features including an improved rangefinder system, enhanced lens mount, and streamlined operation. The Super Graphic featured both focal-plane and lens shutters, offering photographers maximum flexibility in various shooting conditions. Its construction emphasized durability and precision, with improved manufacturing techniques producing tighter tolerances and more reliable mechanisms. The camera became the preferred choice for professional photojournalists who demanded the highest standards of performance and reliability. Many consider the Super Graphic the pinnacle of American press camera design, representing the end of an era before 35mm cameras began dominating news photography.
