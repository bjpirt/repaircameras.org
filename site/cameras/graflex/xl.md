The Graflex XL was a specialized large format camera designed for extreme wide-angle photography and architectural work. This unique camera featured an extra-long bellows extension and enhanced lens mounting system to accommodate the specific requirements of wide-angle lenses on large format film. The XL designation stood for "Extra Long," reflecting its extended capabilities for perspective control and close-up work. The camera incorporated precision movements for front and rear standards, allowing photographers to correct keystoning and achieve optimal depth of field control. Professional architectural and commercial photographers valued the XL for its ability to handle extreme wide-angle lenses while maintaining image quality and geometric accuracy. The robust construction and specialized design made it an essential tool for technical photography requiring precise perspective control and maximum coverage.
