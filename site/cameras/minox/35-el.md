The Minox 35 EL, introduced in 1974, marked Minox's successful transition from subminiature spy cameras to the 35mm format while maintaining their reputation for ultra-compact design. This camera featured a high-quality 35mm f/2.8 lens, electronic exposure control with LED indicators, and measured just 4 inches long. The "EL" designation indicated its electronic light metering system, which provided accurate exposures in various lighting conditions. Despite its tiny size, comparable to a cigarette pack, the 35 EL delivered exceptional image quality that rivaled much larger cameras. It became popular among professionals and enthusiasts who needed a discrete, pocketable camera without compromising on photographic performance or the legendary Minox precision engineering.
