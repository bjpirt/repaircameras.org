The Minox 35 GT, launched in 1979, represented the pinnacle of Minox's 35mm compact camera series, featuring advanced titanium construction that reduced weight while increasing durability. This premium model incorporated a sophisticated 35mm f/2.8 lens with multicoating, electronic flash synchronization, and precise exposure metering in an incredibly compact titanium body. The "GT" designation emphasized its "Gran Turismo" nature—a high-performance travel camera built for demanding photographers. Weighing just 200 grams despite its titanium construction, it offered exceptional image quality and reliability in extreme conditions. The camera became highly sought after by professionals and collectors alike, representing Minox's commitment to combining aerospace-grade materials with their legendary precision manufacturing techniques and optical excellence.
