The Minox 110 S represents the German manufacturer's venture into the popular 110 cartridge film format, departing from their traditional subminiature 8x11mm cassettes. Introduced in the 1970s, this ultra-compact camera maintained Minox's reputation for precision engineering while offering the convenience of readily available 110 film. The camera featured automatic exposure control, a sharp 23mm lens, and the characteristic Minox build quality in an impossibly small package. Despite using the smaller 110 format, the camera delivered surprisingly good image quality for its size, making it popular among travelers and photography enthusiasts who needed maximum portability without sacrificing Minox's legendary craftsmanship and reliability.
