The Fuji GW690 III is a professional medium format rangefinder camera that produces 6x9cm images on 120/220 roll film, earning the nickname "Texas Leica" for its substantial size and exceptional image quality. This camera features a fixed Fujinon 90mm f/3.5 lens equivalent to a 28mm wide-angle in 35mm format, making it ideal for landscape, documentary, and architectural photography. The GW690 III includes a bright, accurate rangefinder focusing system, built-in light meter, and mechanical leaf shutter with speeds from 1 second to 1/400s. Renowned for its sharp optics, solid construction, and the stunning detail possible with its large negative format, the GW690 III became a favorite among professional photographers seeking portable medium format capability without the complexity of interchangeable lens systems.
