The Fuji GX680 is a professional medium format SLR camera system designed specifically for studio photography, producing 6x8cm images on 120/220 roll film. This modular camera features interchangeable lenses, film backs, and viewfinders, with extensive movements including rise, fall, shift, and tilt for perspective control and focus manipulation similar to view cameras. The GX680 system includes electronic shutters, TTL metering, and accepts a range of Fujinon lenses specifically designed for the system. Built like a tank with precision engineering, the GX680 became the standard for high-end commercial photography, fashion, and product photography where image quality, versatility, and precise control were paramount. Its ability to combine medium format quality with view camera movements made it indispensable in professional studios worldwide.
