The Prontor Press Shutter is a professional-grade leaf shutter system designed for large format view cameras and press cameras from the 1930s through 1960s. Manufactured by the German company Gauthier (later Alfred Gauthier GmbH), these shutters were renowned for their precision, reliability, and smooth operation. Features include speeds from 1 second to 1/400s plus time and bulb settings, with synchronized flash contacts for professional photography applications. The Press Shutter's robust construction and precise timing made it the choice of professional photographers, particularly photojournalists who needed dependable equipment under demanding conditions. Its modular design allowed integration with various lens manufacturers and camera systems. The shutter's self-cocking mechanism and reliable flash synchronization supported the fast-paced requirements of press photography. Today, these shutters remain highly valued by large format photographers and camera restorers for their exceptional build quality and smooth, accurate operation across decades of use.
