The Nikon 28Ti is a premium compact 35mm film camera introduced in 1994, featuring a sharp 28mm f/2.8 Nikkor lens in a titanium body. This ultra-compact point-and-shoot camera represented Nikon's answer to high-end compact cameras like the Contax T series. The titanium construction provides exceptional durability while keeping weight minimal, making it ideal for travel photography. Key features include autofocus, automatic exposure with manual override, built-in flash, and a weatherproof design. The 28Ti was targeted at serious photographers seeking professional image quality in an extremely portable package, earning acclaim for its exceptional lens performance and robust build quality in street photography circles.
