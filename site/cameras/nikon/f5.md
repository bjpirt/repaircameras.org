The Nikon F5, launched in 1996, represents the ultimate expression of professional 35mm SLR technology, incorporating cutting-edge autofocus and metering systems. This flagship camera featured a revolutionary 5-segment 3D Color Matrix metering system, lightning-fast autofocus with 5 focus areas, and an impressive 8 fps continuous shooting rate. Built with a magnesium alloy body and comprehensive weather sealing, the F5 was engineered for the most demanding professional applications. Notable features included extensive customization options, compatibility with virtually all Nikkor lenses, and advanced flash control systems. The F5's sophisticated electronics were balanced with intuitive controls, making complex functions easily accessible. This camera became the preferred choice of sports and wildlife photographers, establishing new standards for autofocus speed and accuracy in professional photography until the digital revolution.
