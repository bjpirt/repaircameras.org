The Nikon 35Ti is a luxury compact 35mm film camera launched in 1993, featuring a superb 35mm f/2.8 Nikkor lens housed in a distinctive titanium body. This premium point-and-shoot camera was designed for discerning photographers who demanded exceptional image quality without the bulk of an SLR system. The titanium construction offers superior corrosion resistance and durability while maintaining an elegant appearance that ages beautifully. Notable features include precise autofocus, sophisticated metering, manual exposure override, and a high-quality built-in flash. The 35Ti became highly sought after by street photographers and travel enthusiasts for its discrete operation, outstanding lens sharpness, and virtually indestructible build quality, establishing itself as one of the finest compact cameras ever made.
