The Nikkormat EL, introduced in 1972, was Nikon's first electronic shutter SLR, representing a significant technological advancement in the Nikkormat line. This 35mm camera featured aperture-priority automatic exposure with an innovative electronic shutter system, offering smooth operation across a wide range of speeds. The EL's robust construction incorporated center-weighted through-the-lens metering, a bright viewfinder with LED exposure indicators, and full compatibility with Nikkor lenses. Key features included stepless shutter speeds for precise exposure control, mechanical backup speeds, and the reliability expected from the Nikkormat series. The camera was designed for serious amateur photographers who wanted advanced automatic exposure capabilities without the cost of professional models. The EL successfully combined Nikkormat's legendary build quality with modern electronic convenience, making sophisticated exposure control accessible to enthusiast photographers and establishing electronic shutters as the future direction for SLR development.
