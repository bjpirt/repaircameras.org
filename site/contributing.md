All contributions are welcome! Whether you've found a service manual, want to share a repair tutorial, or just spotted a typo &mdash; we'd love your help building this free resource for camera repairers.

## How to contribute

We have an online editor that makes it easy to add or edit content without any coding knowledge.

- Click the **Edit this page** button on any camera or tutorial page (or go straight to the [editor](/admin/))
- Sign in with a free [GitHub](https://github.com/) account
- Make your edits using the online editor
- Submit your changes for review
- A site maintainer will check everything over and your changes go live

That's it &mdash; no coding required.

## What you can contribute

- **Camera pages** &mdash; add a new camera with links to service manuals, repair guides, and useful resources
- **Repair tutorials** &mdash; write step-by-step guides with annotated photos to help others fix their cameras
- **Service manuals** &mdash; upload scans of service or user manuals as PDFs
- **Links to resources** &mdash; YouTube videos, blog posts, or other repair guides from around the web
- **Improvements** &mdash; fix typos, add troubleshooting tips, or expand existing pages

## For developers

The site is open source. If you prefer working with code directly, you can also contribute via a [pull request on GitHub](https://github.com/bjpirt/repaircameras.org).
