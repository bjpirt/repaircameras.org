Hasselblad 203

✎ Edit this page

The Hasselblad 203FE, introduced in the late 1980s, represented the pinnacle of the company's focal plane shutter camera development. This professional 6x6cm medium format SLR featured an electronically controlled focal plane shutter with exceptional precision and reliability, offering speeds from 32 seconds to 1/2000th second. The camera incorporated advanced electronics with improved metering systems and enhanced weather resistance for professional field use. Built around Hasselblad's legendary modular system, the 203FE accepted the complete range of Carl Zeiss lenses and accessories, making it incredibly versatile for various photography applications. Professional photographers appreciated its robust construction, precise exposure control, and the flexibility of the interchangeable film magazine system. The 203FE became a trusted tool for studio work, fashion photography, and technical applications where uncompromising image quality was essential.
