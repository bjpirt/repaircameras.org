Hasselblad 503cw

✎ Edit this page

The Hasselblad 503CW, introduced in the late 1990s, represented the evolution of the classic V-series medium format SLR system with modern refinements for professional photography. This 6x6cm camera featured the traditional leaf shutter system using Carl Zeiss lenses with built-in shutters, providing excellent flash synchronization at all speeds. The 503CW incorporated improved winding mechanism, enhanced mirror dampening, and refined ergonomics while maintaining the legendary Hasselblad modular system compatibility. Professional photographers valued its whisper-quiet operation, exceptional build quality, and the ability to use the extensive range of V-series lenses and accessories. The camera became particularly popular for studio work, fashion photography, and portrait applications where its smooth operation and impeccable image quality made it an indispensable tool for demanding professional assignments requiring the highest standards of precision and reliability.
