Hasselblad 903swc

✎ Edit this page

The Hasselblad 903SWC was a specialized wide-angle camera designed for architectural, landscape, and technical photography applications requiring exceptional perspective control and edge-to-edge sharpness. This unique 6x6cm medium format camera featured a fixed Carl Zeiss Biogon 38mm f/4.5 lens, renowned for its extraordinary wide-angle coverage with minimal distortion. Unlike traditional SLR cameras, the 903SWC used a rangefinder-style design with an optical viewfinder, eliminating mirror slap for maximum sharpness and quiet operation. The camera incorporated Hasselblad's modular film magazine system, allowing rapid film changes and various film formats. Professional photographers valued its exceptional build quality, precise rangefinder focusing, and the ability to capture expansive scenes with remarkable detail and geometric accuracy, making it indispensable for architectural documentation, interior photography, and artistic applications demanding superior wide-angle performance.
