Nikon FE10

✎ Edit this page

The Nikon FE10, introduced in 1995, was an entry-level manual-focus SLR designed to make traditional photography accessible to budget-conscious enthusiasts and students. This compact 35mm camera featured aperture-priority automatic exposure alongside full manual control, offering the essential functionality of more expensive models at an affordable price. The FE10's simplified design incorporated center-weighted metering, a bright viewfinder with LED exposure indicators, and compatibility with AI and manual Nikkor lenses. Key features included mechanical operation that functioned without batteries (except for metering), reliable shutter speeds from 1 to 1/1000 second, and classic manual-focus operation. The camera was specifically targeted at photography students, beginners learning manual techniques, and enthusiasts seeking a backup body. The FE10 successfully provided access to the renowned Nikon lens system and traditional photographic controls, making it an ideal choice for those learning fundamental photography skills in an increasingly automated world.
