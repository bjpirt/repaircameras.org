Nikon F65

The Nikon F65, introduced in 2001, was an entry-level autofocus SLR designed to offer advanced features in an extremely user-friendly package for amateur photographers. This compact 35mm camera featured 3D Matrix metering, reliable autofocus with focus tracking, and comprehensive automatic exposure modes alongside manual control options. The F65's lightweight construction incorporated a built-in pop-up flash, automatic film handling, and compatibility with AF and AI Nikkor lenses. Key features included various scene modes optimized for different shooting conditions, exposure compensation, and a clear viewfinder with focus confirmation. The camera was specifically targeted at photography newcomers and casual users who wanted access to quality optics and advanced metering without complexity. As one of Nikon's final film SLRs before the digital transition, the F65 represented affordable access to the renowned Nikon system, making professional-quality photography accessible to budget-conscious enthusiasts.
