Nikon Nikkormat FT2

✎ Edit this page

The Nikkormat FT2, launched in 1975, was an improved version of the FTn that offered enhanced metering capabilities and refined handling characteristics. This mechanical 35mm SLR featured center-weighted through-the-lens metering with improved accuracy, full manual exposure control, and the legendary build quality that defined the Nikkormat series. The FT2's robust construction incorporated a bright viewfinder with match-needle exposure display, precise mechanical shutter, and full compatibility with Nikkor lenses. Key improvements over its predecessor included better metering sensitivity, enhanced viewfinder information, and refined controls for easier operation. The camera was designed for serious amateur photographers and professionals seeking a reliable backup body with exceptional durability. The FT2 became highly regarded for its mechanical precision, weather resistance, and virtually indestructible construction, making it a favorite among photographers working in demanding conditions where absolute reliability was essential for capturing important images.
