Nikon F

The Nikon F, introduced in 1959, revolutionized professional 35mm photography and established the blueprint for modern SLR cameras. This groundbreaking camera featured the legendary Nikon F-mount system, which remains compatible with current Nikon lenses today. Built like a tank with a robust mechanical design, the F offered complete manual control, interchangeable viewfinders, and exceptional reliability that made it the choice of photojournalists worldwide. Its modular system allowed photographers to customize the camera for specific needs with various finders, focusing screens, and motor drives. The Nikon F's reputation for durability and precision made it an icon among professional photographers, documenting major historical events throughout the 1960s and beyond, cementing Nikon's reputation in professional photography.
