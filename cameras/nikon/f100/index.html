Nikon F100

✎ Edit this page

The Nikon F100, launched in 1999, represents an exceptional professional 35mm SLR that offered flagship-level performance at a more accessible price point. This camera featured advanced 3D Color Matrix metering, a sophisticated 5-area autofocus system, and 5 fps continuous shooting capabilities in a compact, weather-sealed magnesium alloy body. The F100 incorporated many technologies from the flagship F5, including intelligent scene recognition and extensive customization options, making professional-grade features available to serious enthusiasts. Notable characteristics include excellent ergonomics, bright viewfinder, comprehensive exposure modes, and full compatibility with the vast Nikkor lens system. The F100 became hugely popular among wedding photographers, photojournalists, and advanced amateurs who needed professional performance without the bulk and cost of the F5, establishing itself as one of the most successful semi-professional SLRs ever produced.
