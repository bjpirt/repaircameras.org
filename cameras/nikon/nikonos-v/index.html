Nikon Nikonos V

✎ Edit this page

The Nikonos V, launched in 1984, was the most sophisticated underwater camera in the Nikonos series, featuring advanced TTL flash control and refined exposure systems for professional underwater photography. This specialized 35mm camera maintained waterproof construction rated to 50 meters (160 feet) while incorporating state-of-the-art TTL flash metering that revolutionized underwater flash photography. The Nikonos V featured bright optical viewfinder, sophisticated center-weighted metering, and full compatibility with UW-Nikkor underwater lenses and dedicated underwater flash systems. Key innovations included TTL flash automation, improved manual exposure control, and enhanced weather sealing for exceptional reliability. The camera was designed for professional underwater photographers, marine researchers, and serious diving enthusiasts who demanded the highest image quality and most advanced flash control available. The Nikonos V became the ultimate choice for underwater photography, offering unmatched flash automation and exposure precision that enabled photographers to capture stunning images in the challenging lighting conditions found beneath the surface.
