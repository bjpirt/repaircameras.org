Nikon F3

✎ Edit this page

The Nikon F3, produced from 1980 to 2001, represents the pinnacle of mechanical SLR design and became the most beloved camera among photojournalists worldwide. This professional 35mm SLR featured aperture-priority automatic exposure alongside full manual control, housed in an exceptionally durable titanium-honeycomb body. The F3's bright viewfinder, precise center-weighted metering, and legendary build quality made it the choice of war correspondents and documentary photographers for over two decades. Notable features include interchangeable focusing screens, extensive accessory system compatibility, and the iconic red stripe design. Variants included the F3T (titanium), F3P (press), and F3H (high-speed). The F3's reputation for reliability in extreme conditions and its smooth, quiet operation established it as one of the greatest professional cameras ever made.
