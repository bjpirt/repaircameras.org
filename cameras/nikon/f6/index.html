Nikon F6

The Nikon F6, introduced in 2004, stands as Nikon's final and most advanced 35mm SLR camera, representing the culmination of decades of film camera development. This professional camera incorporated technology from Nikon's digital SLRs, including an 11-area autofocus system, 1005-pixel RGB sensor for precise metering, and advanced scene recognition capabilities. The F6 featured a robust magnesium alloy body with comprehensive weather sealing, maintaining the legendary F-series build quality. Key innovations included intelligent autofocus tracking, sophisticated flash control, and extensive customization options via custom functions. As Nikon's digital flagship, the F6 bridged analog and digital technologies, offering film photographers access to the latest autofocus and metering advancements. Though production ceased in 2020, the F6 remains highly regarded as the ultimate film SLR for professional and enthusiast photographers.
