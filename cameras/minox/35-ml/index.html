Minox 35 ML

✎ Edit this page

The Minox 35 ML, introduced in 1978, enhanced the successful 35mm compact camera line with improved low-light capabilities and refined ergonomics. This model featured a bright 35mm f/2.8 lens optimized for available light photography, sensitive light metering for accurate exposures in challenging conditions, and the characteristic Minox ultra-compact design. The "ML" designation indicated its enhanced light sensitivity and metering improvements over earlier models. Despite measuring just 4 inches in length, the camera delivered professional-quality results thanks to its precision German optics and mechanical engineering. It became particularly popular among photojournalists and street photographers who valued its discrete size, reliable performance, and ability to capture sharp images in low-light situations where larger cameras would be impractical.
