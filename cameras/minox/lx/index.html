Minox LX

✎ Edit this page

The Minox LX represents the modern evolution of the legendary subminiature spy camera series, maintaining the classic 8x11mm film format that made Minox famous in espionage circles. Introduced in the 1990s, this precision-engineered camera features a high-quality 15mm f/3.5 lens, electronic exposure control, and built-in electronic flash in an impossibly compact stainless steel body. The LX continues the tradition of extreme miniaturization that made original Minox cameras essential tools for intelligence agencies worldwide. Despite its tiny size—smaller than most smartphones—the camera delivers remarkable image quality through meticulous German engineering and optical excellence. Popular among collectors, photography enthusiasts, and those requiring ultimate discretion, the LX maintains Minox's reputation for combining cutting-edge technology with uncompromising precision in the world's smallest serious camera format.
