Minox 35 PE

✎ Edit this page

The Minox 35 PE, launched in 1981, represented an advanced evolution of the 35mm compact series with sophisticated electronic features and enhanced automation. This model incorporated programmed electronic exposure control, hence the "PE" designation, which automatically selected optimal aperture and shutter speed combinations for various shooting conditions. The camera featured a high-quality 35mm f/2.8 lens, precise electronic metering, and flash synchronization in the traditional ultra-compact Minox form factor. Its advanced electronics provided consistent, accurate exposures while maintaining the discrete size that made Minox cameras famous. The 35 PE became popular among both amateur and professional photographers who appreciated its technological sophistication combined with the legendary Minox build quality and precision engineering that delivered exceptional image quality from such a tiny package.
