Minox FC 35

✎ Edit this page

The Minox FC 35, introduced in the early 1980s, was designed as a more affordable entry point into the prestigious Minox 35mm camera line while maintaining essential quality standards. This model featured focus-free fixed-focus optics optimized for general photography, automatic exposure control, and a compact design that preserved Minox's signature ultra-portable philosophy. The "FC" designation indicated its fixed-focus convenience, eliminating focusing concerns for casual photographers while ensuring sharp images from approximately 3 feet to infinity. Despite being positioned as a budget-friendly option, the FC 35 retained Minox's commitment to precision manufacturing and reliable performance. It became popular among travelers and casual photographers who wanted the Minox name and quality in a simplified, more accessible package without complex controls.
