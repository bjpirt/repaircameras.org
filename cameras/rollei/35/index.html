Rollei 35

✎ Edit this page

The Rollei 35 is a remarkably compact 35mm viewfinder camera first introduced in 1966, designed by Heinz Waaske and manufactured in Germany. Despite its tiny dimensions, this camera features a high-quality Tessar or Sonnar lens, precise manual focus, and a reliable leaf shutter with speeds from 1/2 to 1/500 second. The camera's innovative design places the film advance lever on the left side and requires the lens to be collapsed for loading film. Its exceptional build quality, sharp optics, and incredibly small size made it a favorite among travel photographers and professionals needing a discrete, portable camera. The Rollei 35 became legendary for proving that superior image quality didn't require a large camera body, establishing a new standard for compact 35mm cameras.
