Chinon CP-7m

✎ Edit this page

The Chinon CP-7M is a 35mm SLR camera introduced in the early 1980s as part of Chinon's affordable camera lineup. This pentaprism camera features manual focus through a split-image rangefinder and microprism collar in its viewfinder, with aperture-priority automatic exposure and full manual mode. The camera accepts K-mount lenses and offers shutter speeds from 1 second to 1/1000s, plus bulb mode. Built with typical Chinon quality, the CP-7M provided amateur photographers with reliable SLR functionality at an accessible price point, making it a popular choice for photography students and enthusiasts seeking manual control without premium costs.
