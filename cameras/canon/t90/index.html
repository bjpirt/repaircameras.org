Canon T90

✎ Edit this page

The Canon T90 is a flagship 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1986, representing the pinnacle of Canon's FD mount system with cutting-edge electronic technology. This advanced camera features multiple exposure modes including program, aperture priority, shutter priority, and manual, with sophisticated TTL metering including spot and center-weighted patterns. The T90 includes innovative features like predictive autofocus, multiple exposure capability, and comprehensive LCD display showing all camera settings. Notable for its ergonomic design and weather sealing, it offers high-speed motor drive and extensive customization options. Targeted at professional and serious amateur photographers, the T90 served as Canon's technological showcase, incorporating many features that would later appear in the EOS system, making it the ultimate expression of mechanical SLR sophistication.
