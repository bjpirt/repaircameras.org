Canon Demi

The Canon Demi is a compact half-frame 35mm camera introduced in 1963, designed to maximize film economy by capturing two images per standard 35mm frame. This innovative camera features a selenium light meter with automatic exposure control and is equipped with a 28mm f/2.8 Canon lens. The Demi offers both automatic and manual exposure settings with shutter speeds ranging from 1/30 to 1/250 second. Targeted at budget-conscious photographers and travelers, its ultra-compact size and ability to capture 72 images on a standard 36-exposure roll made it ideal for casual photography and documentation, representing Canon's foray into the growing half-frame camera market.
