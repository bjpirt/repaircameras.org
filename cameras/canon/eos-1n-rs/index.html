Canon EOS 1N RS

The Canon EOS-1N RS is a specialized professional 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1994, featuring a revolutionary pellicle mirror system that enables continuous viewing during rapid-fire shooting. This unique fixed semi-transparent mirror allows for exceptional autofocus tracking and eliminates viewfinder blackout during the camera's impressive 10 frames per second burst rate. The 1N RS includes advanced 5-area autofocus, multiple exposure modes, and professional-grade weather sealing. Designed specifically for sports and wildlife photographers requiring ultimate speed and tracking performance, it was Canon's flagship action camera, though the pellicle mirror reduced light transmission by approximately one-third of a stop.
