Canon Sureshot Supreme

✎ Edit this page

The Canon Sureshot Supreme is a compact 35mm point-and-shoot camera introduced in the 1990s, designed for casual photographers seeking simplicity and reliability in an automated camera. This camera features fully automatic operation with built-in flash, autofocus, and auto-exposure systems requiring no manual settings. The Sureshot Supreme includes a zoom lens with moderate telephoto capability and programmed exposure modes optimized for various shooting conditions. Notable for its user-friendly operation and compact size, it offers one-touch photography with sophisticated automatic features. Targeted at families and recreational photographers who prioritized convenience over manual control, the Sureshot Supreme represented Canon's commitment to making quality photography accessible to everyone regardless of technical knowledge.
