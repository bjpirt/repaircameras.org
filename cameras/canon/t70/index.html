Canon T70

✎ Edit this page

The Canon T70 is an advanced amateur 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1984, featuring sophisticated automatic exposure modes and electronic controls. This camera offers program, aperture priority, and shutter priority modes with TTL center-weighted and spot metering options. The T70 includes Canon's innovative Automatic Selection (AS) mode that chooses appropriate exposure settings based on focal length and shooting conditions. Notable for its comprehensive LCD display panel and motorized film advance/rewind, it accepts Canon FD lenses and features multiple exposure capability. Targeted at enthusiast photographers seeking advanced automation with creative control options, the T70 represented Canon's push toward electronic SLR innovation while maintaining compatibility with the established FD lens system.
