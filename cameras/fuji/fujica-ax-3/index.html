Fuji Fujica AX-3

✎ Edit this page

The Fujica AX-3 represents an enhanced version in Fuji's AX series of 35mm SLR cameras, building upon the foundation established by the AX-1 with additional features and refined functionality. This camera maintains the aperture-priority automatic exposure system with improved LED viewfinder display, manual exposure override, and compatibility with Fujinon X-mount lenses. The AX-3 features enhanced metering capabilities, improved shutter mechanism with speeds from 1 second to 1/1000s plus bulb, and often included additional exposure modes or refinements over its predecessor. Designed for advancing amateur photographers, the AX-3 offered reliable performance, solid construction, and access to Fuji's renowned lens ecosystem during the peak years of 35mm SLR popularity.
