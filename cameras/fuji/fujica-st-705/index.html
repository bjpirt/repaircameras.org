Fuji Fujica ST-705

✎ Edit this page

The Fujica ST-705 is a 35mm SLR camera from Fuji's earlier ST series, representing the company's entry into the competitive SLR market of the early 1970s. This camera features full manual exposure control with a built-in TTL (through-the-lens) light meter, accepting M42 screw-mount lenses which provided compatibility with a wide range of manufacturers' optics. The ST-705 offers a mechanical focal plane shutter with speeds typically ranging from 1 second to 1/1000s, plus bulb mode for long exposures. With its solid construction, reliable meter system, and the versatility of the M42 mount, the ST-705 appealed to photography enthusiasts seeking manual control and lens flexibility during the era when SLR cameras were becoming mainstream among serious photographers.
