Mamiya Seiko Shutter

✎ Edit this page

The Mamiya Seiko Shutter represents a crucial component in Mamiya's professional lens systems, particularly for their medium format cameras. These precision leaf shutters, manufactured by Seiko, were integrated into Mamiya lenses to provide reliable, accurate timing and flash synchronization capabilities across all shutter speeds. Unlike focal plane shutters found in most SLRs, leaf shutters operate within the lens assembly, enabling flash synchronization at any speed—a critical advantage for studio photography. The Seiko mechanism offers shutter speeds typically ranging from 1 second to 1/500, with exceptional consistency and minimal vibration. These shutters became integral to Mamiya's reputation for professional reliability, particularly in the RB67 and RZ67 systems where precise exposure control and dependable operation were essential for commercial and portrait photography applications.
