Mamiya MSX

The Mamiya MSX, introduced in 1972, was one of Mamiya's early ventures into the 35mm SLR market, showcasing their commitment to precision engineering across all camera formats. This mechanical SLR featured a robust metal body construction with shutter speeds ranging from 1 second to 1/1000, plus bulb mode for long exposures. The MSX incorporated through-the-lens metering with a match-needle system and offered full manual control for creative exposure settings. Compatible with the high-quality Mamiya/Sekor lens system, it provided exceptional optical performance that rivaled established 35mm brands. While primarily targeting serious amateur photographers, the MSX demonstrated Mamiya's engineering excellence and attention to detail that made their medium format cameras legendary among professionals.
