Pentax MZ-5

The Pentax MZ-5 is a compact, feature-rich autofocus 35mm SLR camera introduced in 1995. Despite its small size, it offers sophisticated exposure control including 6-segment honeycomb pattern metering, predictive autofocus, and comprehensive shooting modes from full auto to manual. The camera features Pentax's unique hyper-manual and hyper-program modes, allowing seamless transitions between automatic and manual control. Its lightweight plastic construction makes it highly portable while maintaining good ergonomics and a clear viewfinder display. Compatible with the full range of K-mount lenses, the MZ-5 provides excellent value for enthusiast photographers. The camera's combination of advanced features, compact size, and affordability made it popular among students and hobbyists seeking professional capabilities in a travel-friendly package. Its reliable autofocus and accurate metering established it as an excellent entry point into the Pentax system.
