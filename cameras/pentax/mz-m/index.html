Pentax MZ-M

✎ Edit this page

The Pentax MZ-M is a unique manual-focus-only 35mm SLR camera introduced in 2001, designed specifically for photographers who preferred traditional manual operation. Based on the MZ-5 body, it features center-weighted and spot metering modes with aperture-priority and manual exposure control, deliberately omitting autofocus mechanisms. The camera includes essential features like depth-of-field preview, multiple exposure capability, and a bright viewfinder optimized for manual focusing. Its compact, lightweight design appeals to street photographers and purists who value the tactile experience of manual photography. Compatible with all K-mount lenses, the MZ-M provides excellent value for those seeking a modern manual camera with reliable metering. This camera filled a niche for photographers wanting contemporary reliability and ergonomics while maintaining complete manual control over focus and exposure settings.
