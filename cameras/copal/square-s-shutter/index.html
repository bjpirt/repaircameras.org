Copal Square S Shutter

✎ Edit this page

The Copal Square S shutter represents an innovative departure from traditional round leaf shutters, featuring a distinctive square aperture design optimized for specific optical applications. This precision mechanical shutter maintains Copal's renowned reliability with speeds from 1 second to 1/500s, plus bulb and time settings, while its square opening provides unique characteristics for certain lens designs and specialized photography applications. The Square S was particularly favored for process cameras, reproduction work, and scientific photography where the square aperture format offered advantages in image quality and light distribution. Its robust construction and precise timing made it a specialized tool for professional applications requiring exact exposure control and consistent performance.
