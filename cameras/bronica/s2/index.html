Bronica S2

✎ Edit this page

The Bronica S2 is a professional medium format SLR camera introduced in 1965, representing the second generation of Bronica's 6x6cm camera system. This robust camera features a focal plane shutter with speeds from 1s to 1/1000s plus Bulb mode, and accepts the extensive range of Nikkor lenses through its proprietary mount. The S2 offers interchangeable viewfinders, focusing screens, and film backs, providing versatility for various photographic applications. Built with precision mechanics and solid construction, the camera includes a waist-level viewfinder as standard and supports various prism finders. The S2 became popular among professional photographers seeking a reliable, affordable alternative to German medium format systems.
