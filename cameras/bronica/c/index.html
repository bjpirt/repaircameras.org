Bronica C

The Bronica C is a professional medium format SLR camera introduced in 1965 as an evolution of the original Bronica S series. This 6x6cm format camera features interchangeable lenses, viewfinders, and film backs, making it highly versatile for studio and field photography. The focal plane shutter offers speeds from 1s to 1/1000s plus Bulb mode, with flash synchronization at all speeds. The camera accepts Nikkor lenses through its proprietary mount and includes a waist-level viewfinder as standard. Built for professional use, the Bronica C provided an affordable alternative to Hasselblad and Rollei systems while maintaining excellent image quality.
