Minolta 7000

✎ Edit this page

The Minolta 7000 (Maxxum 7000 in North America) was a revolutionary 35mm SLR camera that fundamentally changed photography when introduced in 1985. As the world's first successful integrated autofocus SLR system, it featured a built-in autofocus motor in the camera body that drove compatible lenses through mechanical coupling. The camera offered programmed auto, aperture-priority, shutter-priority, and manual exposure modes, with a focal plane shutter ranging from 30 seconds to 1/2000 second. Its innovative design included predictive autofocus tracking and a comprehensive flash system. The 7000's groundbreaking autofocus technology sparked industry-wide adoption and made Minolta a leader in SLR innovation, appealing to both enthusiasts and professionals seeking cutting-edge photographic technology.
