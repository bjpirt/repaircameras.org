Minolta Dynax 303si

✎ Edit this page

The Minolta Dynax 303si was an entry-level 35mm autofocus SLR camera introduced in the early 1990s as part of Minolta's second-generation autofocus system. This compact and lightweight camera featured improved autofocus speed and accuracy compared to earlier models, along with a simplified control layout ideal for beginners. It offered programmed auto exposure, portrait and landscape scene modes, and a built-in flash with automatic pop-up operation. The 303si used Minolta's A-mount lens system and included features like auto film loading, advance, and rewind. Designed for amateur photographers seeking reliable autofocus performance without complexity, the 303si provided an affordable entry point into SLR photography with modern conveniences that made it particularly appealing to those transitioning from compact cameras.
