Ricoh XF-30

✎ Edit this page

The Ricoh XF-30 is a compact 35mm autofocus camera introduced in the late 1980s, representing Ricoh's entry into the consumer point-and-shoot market. This camera features a built-in 35mm f/3.5 lens with autofocus capability, automatic exposure control, and a built-in flash with red-eye reduction. The XF-30 incorporates DX film speed reading, automatic film loading and advance, and a simple user interface designed for casual photographers. Its lightweight plastic construction and automated features made it an attractive option for family photography and travel. The camera exemplifies the shift toward fully automatic compact cameras that dominated the consumer market in the 1980s and 1990s, offering reliable performance with minimal user input required for successful image capture.
