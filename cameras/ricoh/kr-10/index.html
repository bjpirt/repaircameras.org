Ricoh KR-10

The Ricoh KR-10 is a 35mm single-lens reflex camera introduced in 1978 as part of Ricoh's K-mount system. This entry-level SLR features manual focus with a bright split-image rangefinder focusing screen, manual exposure control, and a top shutter speed of 1/1000 second. The camera accepts standard Pentax K-mount lenses, making it compatible with a wide range of affordable optics. Built with a solid metal construction and simple, reliable mechanics, the KR-10 was designed for amateur photographers seeking an accessible entry into SLR photography. Its straightforward operation, lack of complex electronics, and budget-friendly price point made it popular among students and hobbyists during the late 1970s and early 1980s.
