Graflex Pacemaker Crown

The Graflex Pacemaker Crown Graphic was a lightweight, versatile 4x5 press camera that became the workhorse of mid-20th century photojournalism. Introduced in the 1940s, this camera featured a simplified design compared to the Speed Graphic, omitting the focal-plane shutter to reduce weight and complexity while maintaining professional image quality. The Crown Graphic relied exclusively on lens-mounted shutters, making it more affordable and easier to maintain. Its rigid construction, precise rangefinder system, and excellent lens selection made it ideal for news photography, where portability and reliability were paramount. The camera's distinctive art deco styling and robust build quality established it as an icon of American press photography, documenting decades of historical events from World War II through the civil rights era.
