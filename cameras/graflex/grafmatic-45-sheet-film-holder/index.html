Graflex Grafmatic 45 Sheet Film Holder

The Graflex Grafmatic 45 Sheet Film Holder was the larger format version of the innovative rapid-change film magazine system, designed for 4x5 inch cameras. This remarkable accessory contained twelve sheets of large format film in a single holder, allowing photographers to cycle through exposures by simply pulling a metal tab to advance to the next sheet. The 4x5 Grafmatic became legendary among press photographers who needed to capture multiple shots quickly without the delays of changing individual film holders. The precision-engineered mechanism featured intricate spring systems and light-proof seals to ensure reliable operation in demanding conditions. This innovation transformed professional photography workflows, enabling the rapid documentation of news events and making the Speed Graphic camera system even more versatile and efficient for field work.
