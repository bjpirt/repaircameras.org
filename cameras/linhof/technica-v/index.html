Linhof Technica V

✎ Edit this page

The Linhof Technica V, produced from 1963 to 1978, was a precision German-made large format view camera that set the standard for professional field photography. This 4x5 inch camera featured Linhof's renowned build quality with extensive front and rear movements including rise, fall, shift, tilt, and swing adjustments for perspective control and depth of field manipulation. The Technica V incorporated a coupled rangefinder system for handheld use, making it unique among large format cameras for its versatility between studio and field work. With its robust all-metal construction, precise focusing mechanisms, and ability to accept a wide range of lenses, the Technica V became the preferred choice for architectural, landscape, and commercial photographers who demanded the ultimate in image quality and camera movements.
